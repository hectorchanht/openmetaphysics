The most underestimated, most needed subject of the AI era.

Every AI question is secretly a metaphysics question wearing a lab coat. OpenMetaphysics asks the questions the other halls quietly assume.

Status: Founding — initialized, first release in preparation

What it is

What is real? (simulations, generated media, canon vs copies) What is a mind? (models, agents) What exists? (data, forms, sources) What is identity over time? (versions, canon) What is truth? (verification, labels) The ancients built the vocabulary — being, form, substance, cause — and the AI era is re-asking every one of their questions at industrial scale. OpenMetaphysics is the root hall under OpenSchool: epistemology is its method wing, and the founding-night corpus itself is its first set of case studies.

END GOAL

Add metaphysics to the curriculum immediately, across Dawn — as its own hall, OpenMetaphysics, and through OpenBook, OpenAcademy, OpenStudy and OpenTeacher — so the AI era relearns the vocabulary for being, form, substance and cause.
